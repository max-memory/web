Why it helps

Give your relationships some continuity.

  • ✓A briefing from your own notes. Recall roles, birthdays, preferences, family details, and previous conversations—about one person or a relevant group.
  • ✓Capture without a form. Talk, type, or share a business-card photo with a note about how you met.
  • ✓Check the source. Inspect the original memory and correct a name or detail when needed.
  • ✓Keep a conference together. Collect introductions, notes, and photos into a Memory Event.
