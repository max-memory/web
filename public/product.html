Private by architecture

Trust you can inspect, not a promise in a policy.

Max's privacy isn't a toggle we could quietly flip. The product is built so your personal memories stay yours.

  • ✓Your personal memories live only on your iPhone. Stored there, searched there. There is no cloud memory database to breach, subpoena, or mine.
  • ✓No account, no profile. Max doesn't know your email, your name, or your phone number — there's nothing to sign up for. Even the subscription, when it arrives, runs through the App Store: payment without identity.
  • ✓Max only knows what you tell it. It never reads your mail, mines your messages, or tracks your location in the background. Deliberate memory — tell, don't observe.
  • ✓Secrets are a different class of memory. Entered behind a lock button, never spoken aloud by default, never in home-screen search, never through Siri. Revealed only on screen, only when you ask.
  • ✓Photos, contacts, and calendars stay in their apps. Max holds references, never copies — your photo library and address book remain the single source of truth.
  • ✓What travels, travels honestly. An online chat sends your question and the few memories needed to answer it to the AI model — for that turn, and that's all. Conversation history is stored for you as sealed data our servers can't read.
  • ✓No ads, no tracking, ever. The app declares zero tracking to Apple. This website loads nothing from third parties — no analytics, no fonts, no pixels.

On the roadmap: encrypted backup, so your memories survive a lost phone — leaving your iPhone only as ciphertext that only you can unlock. And later, shared memories your household keeps across its phones. Your personal memories stay on yours, always.
