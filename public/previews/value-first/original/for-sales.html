Why it fits

Relationship memory, not another CRM.

  • ✓Cards that read like a briefing. Role and company first, then family, preferences, history — the way you'd actually prep.
  • ✓No fields, no forms, no admin. You talk; Max files. The debrief happens before you've left the parking lot.
  • ✓Plays nicely with Contacts. Link a person to their contact card and their number is a tap away — details stay in Contacts, never copied out.

  • ✓Discovery across everyone. "Who mentioned budget season?" "Which clients are in Denver?" Your accumulated knowledge finally answers back.
  • ✓Conferences, kept whole. Make the trade show a Memory Event — the intros, the session notes, the badge photos, gathered in one place and asked for together.
  • ✓Your book stays yours. These are your relationships, on your phone — not rows in an employer's database.
