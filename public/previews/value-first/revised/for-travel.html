Why it helps

Keep the useful parts of a trip close.

  • ✓Places with your reasons attached. Share a location from Maps and add why you want to remember it.
  • ✓A trip kept together. Use a Memory Event for its notes, places, photos, and videos.
  • ✓Hands-on access offline. Browse your memories and edit lists with the radios off. On-device conversation is more limited than online conversation.
  • ✓An easy way to pass a place along. Open a saved place for directions or share it through your phone’s share sheet.
