For caregivers

Less to hold while you care.

Shared reminders and calendar appointments with alerts can prompt Mom on her iPhone when it’s time. Keep appointment details, pharmacy notes, and the everyday information you both need within reach.

Shared spaces · planned Premium offering

Help Mom know
when it’s time.

In your shared space, ask Max for a reminder with a specific time. Once Mom’s phone syncs, it creates a reminder in her Apple Reminders app, ready to notify her when it’s time.

She doesn’t have to remember to ask Max or have Max open when the scheduled alert appears. You can keep the appointment’s address and entrance details in the same shared space for either of you to find.

Shared reminders can notify each member who has reminders enabled for that space, including you.

Setup on each phone: allow Max access to Reminders, leave the space’s reminders enabled, and sync before the reminder is due. Alerts follow that phone’s notification and Focus settings.

A calendar alert for somewhere to be

The appointment belongs on the shared calendar. With that calendar available on Mom’s phone and an alert configured for her, it can notify her before the appointment.

Sharing a calendar appointment does not guarantee that everyone gets an alert. Check the alert on Mom’s calendar as well as her phone’s notification settings. Each member’s calendar alerts can differ.

Why it helps

Details you choose. Information you can check.

  • ✓Deliberate capture. Max remembers what you give it; it does not monitor someone’s messages or inbox.
  • ✓A source to refer back to. Review the original words and when you recorded them.
  • ✓A time to act. Ask for a reminder and name a time to get an Apple Reminders alert. Simply saving a task in Max does not schedule a notification.
  • ✓A separate personal store. Personal memories are stored on the phone. Online conversations process relevant context through an AI provider.

Max is a memory helper, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, or make care decisions. Check important details against their original source.
