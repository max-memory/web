Personal by design

You choose what Max remembers.

Tell it the things you want to keep. Max doesn’t read your inbox or monitor your messages. Your personal memory store lives on your iPhone.

Online conversations send your messages and relevant context, which can include recalled memories, through our service to an AI provider. Speech recognition and spoken replies run on your phone.

  • ✓No account to create. No email address or password to get started.
  • ✓Your choices stay visible. Browse, correct, and delete saved memories.
  • ✓Useful without a connection. Browse and edit locally; supported on-device answering has a smaller set of capabilities.
  • ✓No ads or tracking. This website loads no third-party scripts, fonts, or pixels.
