For busy minds

Your brain has better things to do.

When a thought arrives, give it somewhere to stay. Max helps you capture the things you want to remember and find them again in your own words.

A day with Max

Before it slips away

Say where something went while you’re putting it away.

Remember I put the passports in the gray filing box.
Saved — the passports are in the gray filing box.

When you need it

You don’t have to reproduce the sentence you used to save it.

Where on earth are the passports?
In the gray filing box.

When only part of it comes back

Ask with the context you do remember.

What did I need for the science project?
You saved poster board and AA batteries for the science project.

Why it helps

Make remembering a smaller task.

  • ✓Several ways to catch a thought. Talk, type, use Siri, or send something from the share sheet.
  • ✓Ask with imperfect wording. Search by the meaning of what you saved, including names and related details.
  • ✓Choose when you need a nudge. Keep a task in Max, or explicitly ask for an iOS Reminder.
  • ✓Correct things as you go. Review your saved words and fix a name, date, or misplaced detail.
