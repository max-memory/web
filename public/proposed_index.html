The whole idea

Your personal memories live only on your iPhone.

Not on our servers. Not in a cloud database. Max stores and searches your memories on your phone — that isn't a setting, it's the architecture.

  • ✓No account. No email, no password, nothing to sign up for — and when Max grows a subscription, it'll run through the App Store, so we still won't know who you are.
  • ✓Photos stay in your photo library. Max remembers which ones matter and why — it never uploads or copies them.
  • ✓Listening and speaking happen on your phone. Speech recognition and Max's voice both run on-device.
  • ✓Secrets go behind a lock. Door codes and combinations are never spoken aloud and never show up in search.
  • ✓No ads. No tracking. Not in the app, and not on this website either.

The honest fine print: when you chat with Max online, your question and the few memories needed to answer it are sent to the AI model for that turn — and that's all that ever travels.
