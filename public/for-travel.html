Why it fits

Made for people in motion.

  • ✓Offline is the default plan. Your memories are on your phone, so a dead zone, a foreign SIM mess, or a transatlantic flight changes nothing.
  • ✓Parked cars, found. One sentence saves the spot — airport garages, rental lots, that street in a city you don't know.
  • ✓Places worth returning to. Share a spot from Maps with a note; get directions back to it years later, on any continent.
  • ✓Trips stay whole. Confirmation details, room numbers, photos, and "next time, skip this hotel" — collected as one event per trip.
  • ✓Send a place anywhere. "Share the restaurant with Ana" pre-loads the share sheet — to a friend, a group chat, or your car.
